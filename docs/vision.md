# Vision

## Why TopGun Trading exists

Most trading software is loud. It either pretends to print money, hides itself
behind black-box automation, or buries the trader in features they never use.
At the other end, plain charting websites and note-taking apps offer no
discipline, no replay, no review, and no memory.

**TopGun Trading is built for the trader who is trying to get serious.**

It is a workspace for:

- studying the market
- practicing patterns
- replaying sessions
- recording and reviewing trades
- analyzing decisions with AI
- enforcing personal trading rules
- building durable habits

It is not a casino, not a signal feed, not a guarantee. It is a craft tool.

---

## Audience

- Self-directed retail traders who want to professionalize.
- Funded-account candidates working through evaluation programs.
- Students of price action who want a real review and replay loop.
- Long-time traders who want a cleaner, more disciplined daily workflow.

We are **not** building for:

- people looking for "AI bots that print money"
- copy-trading communities
- pump groups
- anyone seeking guaranteed returns

---

## Product principles

1. **Discipline over dopamine.** The product should make a trader calmer, not
   more reactive.
2. **Fewer, sharper features.** Every feature must justify its place. No
   feature bloat to look impressive.
3. **Honest UX.** Mocked or simulated data is always labeled. Probability is
   never sold as certainty.
4. **Premium feel.** Dark, dense, fast, professional. No childish gradients,
   no hype banners, no countdown timers.
5. **Workspace, not feed.** The product is a workspace the trader returns to,
   not a doomscroll surface.
6. **Compose, do not lock in.** Market data, charts, and AI providers are
   abstracted so the platform survives any one vendor change.
7. **Local-first where possible.** Journals and notes belong to the user.
8. **Compliance is a feature.** We are clear about what we are and what we
   are not.

---

## Brand feel

- elite, sharp, modern, fast
- aerospace and aviation cues without being a costume
- monochrome dark base with a single high-contrast accent
- precise typography, generous whitespace inside dense layouts
- voice: confident, calm, technical, never boastful

---

## Success looks like

- A trader opens TopGun Trading every morning before the open and every
  evening after the close.
- Their journal is filling up with real entries they wrote because the tool
  made it easy.
- Their replay sessions are improving their pattern recognition.
- Their AI review is catching mistakes they would have missed.
- Their rule engine is stopping them from breaking their own rules.
- They feel that the product respects them.

That is the bar.
