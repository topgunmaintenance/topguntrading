# Compliance

TopGun Trading is a software workspace for analysis, practice, journaling, and
review. It is **not** an investment advisor, broker-dealer, or money manager.
This document describes the compliance posture we hold ourselves to.

## Plain-language disclaimers

The product surfaces and the marketing site MUST display, in clear language:

- TopGun Trading does not provide personalized investment advice.
- TopGun Trading does not execute orders. It is not a broker-dealer.
- Past performance, replays, or simulations do not predict future results.
- All trading involves risk of loss, including total loss of capital.
- AI commentary is generated by language models and may be incorrect.
- Users are solely responsible for their trading decisions.

These disclaimers must be visible in:

- The marketing site footer
- The signup flow (with explicit acknowledgement)
- The first-run tour of the app shell
- Any AI feature surface (inline near the AI output)
- The terms of service

## What we will not do

- We will not present AI output as a recommendation, signal, or instruction.
- We will not display "win rate" claims that are not user-derived from their
  own journal.
- We will not market guaranteed returns, guaranteed alpha, or "AI that
  prints money."
- We will not encourage users to break their broker's terms of service.
- We will not scrape private broker session data as the primary architecture
  for live data.
- We will not store user broker credentials.

## Data and privacy

- Personal data and journal contents belong to the user. Export must be
  available.
- Third-party providers (market data, AI, payments) are listed in the
  privacy notice.
- See [`docs/security.md`](security.md) for the security posture.

## Region awareness

The product will, over time, vary disclaimers and feature visibility by
jurisdiction. The compliance layer must be designed to support per-region
gating. Until we ship a region layer, we default to the most restrictive set
of disclaimers.

## AI compliance

- Every AI surface must be labeled with the model and provider.
- Outputs must include a "this is not advice" inline disclaimer.
- High-stakes outputs (trade review summaries) must record the prompt
  version and inputs in `AIInteraction` for traceability.
- Prompts live in `packages/ai-prompts` and are versioned. See
  [`docs/ai-strategy.md`](ai-strategy.md).

## Market data compliance

- Each provider has different redistribution and display rules.
- The market data layer must enforce attribution, delay, and entitlement
  rules per provider. See [`docs/market-data-strategy.md`](market-data-strategy.md).
