# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TopGun Trading is built by people who take their craft seriously and
respect each other. We expect everyone working on this repo — employees,
contractors, contributors, and AI agents acting on behalf of any of the
above — to behave the same way.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct without being cruel.
- Disagree on the work, not the person.
- Assume the other person is trying to do the right thing until proven
  otherwise.
- Give credit where it is due.
- Write things down so the next person can pick them up.
- Ask for help when you need it. Offer help when you can.
- Treat user data and trader trust as sacred.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender identity or
  expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, or
  national origin.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or work.
- Encouraging others to do any of the above.

## Scope

This code applies anywhere the project happens: this repository, issue
trackers, pull requests, chat channels, video calls, and any in-person
or virtual events tied to the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to: conduct@topguntrading.example

Reports are read by the project owner. Reporters are protected from
retaliation. Confidentiality is maintained to the extent possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this
code. Possible responses include private warnings, public warnings,
temporary or permanent removal from the project, and removal of
contributions where necessary.
